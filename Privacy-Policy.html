FlareHabit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pavel Koyfman, an individual developer based in Riga, Latvia ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you use the FlareHabit mobile app for Android (the "App"). We are the data controller. Contact: pavel4567@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google we receive your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile photo URL through Firebase Authentication. An account is required to create goals and sync your progress.
  • Content you enter: goal descriptions and answers you give while setting up a goal, generated stages, actions and habits, habit completions, notes, achievements and related progress data.
  • Subscription data: your subscription status, product, purchase dates and an app user identifier linked to your account. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive or store your card or other payment details.
  • Usage and device data: app interaction events, app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate country derived from IP address (Firebase/Google Analytics), plus crash reports and diagnostic logs (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • App integrity data: device attestation tokens used to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the App (Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity).
  • Settings stored on your device: preferences and reminder schedules. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

We do not collect your precise or approximate GPS location, contacts, photos or files, and the App does not show advertising.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the App: create and sync your goals, stages, habits and progress across sessions and devices (performance of a contract).
  • To generate personalised goal plans, stages, actions and habits from the goal text and answers you provide (performance of a contract).
  • To provide and verify Premium subscriptions (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the App secure and reliable, diagnose crashes and fix bugs (legitimate interests).
  • To understand how the App is used in aggregate and improve it (legitimate interests).
  • To answer your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Generated plans and Google Gemini

When you create or continue a goal, the goal text, your setup answers and summary facts about your previous stage progress are sent to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic to generate your plan. Please do not include sensitive personal information (for example health details about other people) in your goal text. Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase and Google Cloud terms.

4. Service providers (processors)

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, Remote Config, Firebase AI Logic / Gemini) – Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC – https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy, https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play (app distribution, billing and subscriptions) – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription management) – https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Unsplash (stock images shown in the App; image requests are made to Unsplash servers, which may see your IP address) – https://unsplash.com/privacy

5. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and associated content are deleted from our active systems within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase retention settings (Analytics: up to 14 months; Crashlytics: about 90 days). Subscription transaction records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for accounting and legal purposes.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data in the App under Settings > Delete account. You can also request deletion without the App by following the instructions at https://pavelk2254.github.io/flarehabit-docs/delete-account.html. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription – cancel it in Google Play to stop future charges.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email pavel4567@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, https://www.dvi.gov.lv) or the authority in your country of residence.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, up to 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with access restricted to your account. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App.
