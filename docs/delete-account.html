Delete your FlareHabit account

FlareHabit – Pavel Koyfman, an individual developer based in Riga, Latvia

You can delete your FlareHabit account and associated data at any time.

Option 1: In the app

  • Open FlareHabit and sign in.
  • Go to Settings > Delete account.
  • Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: Request by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email pavel4567@gmail.com with the subject "FlareHabit account deletion request" from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in. We may ask you to confirm the request.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Google sign-in identifier, email, name, profile photo URL)
  • Your goals, stages, actions, habits, completions, notes, achievements and progress
  • Your subscription link between your account and RevenueCat

What may be kept

  • Purchase and transaction records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting
  • Aggregated or pseudonymous analytics and crash reports, kept for their standard retention period

Timeframe

Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request.

Note: deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

See also our Privacy Policy.
